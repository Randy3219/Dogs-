# Monroe Community College Admissions FAQ

Below are quick answers to common questions about applying to Monroe Community College (MCC). This FAQ is a starting point; always confirm details on the official MCC website.

## Admissions Basics

### How do I apply to MCC?
Applications are submitted online through MCC's admissions portal. You'll create an account, complete the application form, and upload any required documents.

### When are application deadlines?
Deadlines vary by term and program. Fall and spring have different timelines, so check the current admissions calendar for the exact dates.

### What is MCC's acceptance rate?
The sample dataset in this repository (`data/mcc_admissions.csv`) shows acceptance rates around 60%. Actual rates change year to year based on the applicant pool.

### Are there program-specific requirements?
Yes. Some programs require extra materials or prerequisites. Review the program page or contact the admissions office for guidance.

## Costs & Support

### Where can I find information about financial aid?
MCC offers grants, scholarships, and loans. Visit the financial aid section of the MCC website or speak with an advisor for personalized help.

### Who can I contact with questions?
If you're unsure where to start, contact the admissions office for the most accurate and up-to-date guidance.
