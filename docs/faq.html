Admissions Basics

How do I apply to MCC?

Applications are submitted online through MCC's admissions portal. You'll create an account, complete the form, and upload any required documents.

When are application deadlines?

Deadlines vary by term and program. Fall and spring have different timelines, so check the current admissions calendar for exact dates.

What is MCC's acceptance rate?

The sample dataset in this repository shows acceptance rates around 60%. Actual rates change year to year based on the applicant pool.

Are there program-specific requirements?

Yes. Some programs require extra materials or prerequisites. Review the program page or contact the admissions office for guidance.

Costs & Support

Where can I find information about financial aid?

MCC offers grants, scholarships, and loans. Visit the financial aid section of the MCC website or speak with an advisor for personalized help.

Who can I contact with questions?

If you're unsure where to start, contact the admissions office for the most accurate and up-to-date guidance.
